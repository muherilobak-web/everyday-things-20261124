## The entry

> A queue is one long, slow animal made of people, shuffling forward one tiny step at a time toward the front.
>
> It works because a queue really does move in single file like one body, but it leaves out that every part is a person with somewhere to be, growing bored and impatient while the front is a real door, not a head.

## Why this analogy

A queue was chosen over rain or a traffic jam because the picture is one a 10-year-old can stand inside: a whole line of people shuffling forward together really does look and feel like a single slow creature, and the join is surprising enough that nobody in the field has used it. The second line earns its keep by being honest instead of praising the image — it names exactly what the analogy covers, the single-file order and the slow forward shuffle, and exactly what it leaves out, that every segment is a person with somewhere to be who grows bored and impatient, and that the front is a real door rather than the head of an animal. The pair works because the second sentence explains the first rather than repeating it, in plain language a child can follow.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (no trailing newline). No dependencies, no network, no input — deterministic, so two runs produce byte-identical output.
